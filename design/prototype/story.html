我们在支付流水系统里跑 SQLite 已经四年了，峰值每天 3000 万次写入。最关键的决策是 WAL + 每小时 checkpoint，然后备份直接用 litestream 推到 S3，故障恢复从未超过两分钟。
We've been running SQLite in our payments ledger for four years now, peaking at 30M writes/day. The key decision was WAL + hourly checkpoints, with litestream shipping backups to S3 — recovery has never taken more than two minutes.
